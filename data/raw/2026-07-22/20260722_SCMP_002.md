# China, EU pushing ahead on joint research despite trade tensions

### Joint 2025-2027 research agreement covers climate and agriculture, state media says, signalling will to collaborate in spite of differences

Yuanyue Dang in Beijing

Published: 10:25pm, 22 Jul 2026  
Updated: 11:21pm, 22 Jul 2026

China and the EU have renewed their joint research agreement, according to Chinese state-linked media, signalling a willingness to collaborate on climate and agriculture despite trade tensions.

People’s Financial News, a publication affiliated with Communist Party mouthpiece People’s Daily, reported on the new two-year agreement on Wednesday.

China’s Ministry of Science and Technology and the European Commission had signed joint research funding agreements to support scientific cooperation through to 2027, the report said.

The cooperation focuses on areas such as food, agriculture and biosolutions, as well as climate change and biodiversity.

The report did not say when the agreement was signed.

China and the European Union have been trying to find common ground despite lingering tensions over trade, Chinese-made electric vehicles flooding the European market and the Russia-Ukraine war.
